Game regulations

To keep our community safe, welcoming, and enjoyable for everyone, we ask that all members follow the rules below while in the Florida State Roleplay Game Rules. Failure to follow these rules may result in warnings, removal from the game, or bans from the game.

Rule 1 | Random Deathmatch (RDM)

Random Deathmatch occurs when a player kills, shoots, punches, or attacks another player without a valid in character reason. All acts of violence must have a proper roleplay justification and context.

Rule 2 | Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

Vehicle Deathmatch is when a player uses a vehicle to intentionally harm, ram, or collide with another player without a valid in character reason. Accidents must be roleplayed realistically, and reckless vehicle usage for no purpose is prohibited. Excessive ramming or intentional collisions will result in disciplinary action.

Rule 3 | Fail Roleplay (FRP) & Avatars

Fail Roleplay occurs when a player acts in a way that breaks immersion or realism. Examples include surviving impossible falls, ignoring injuries, pulling unrealistic stunts, or interfering with a scene you are not part of..

Your roblox avatar may not resemble a child due to roblox content restrictions, Any roleplays involving minors below -13 is not allowed due to it being restricted and you may receive a kick or a ban depending on the severity.

Animal roleplay is not allowed whatsoever, Caught acting or cosplaying as an animal will result in up to a 30 minute kick from the server.

Rule 4 | New Life Rule

The New Life Rule requires that after your character dies, you must forget all previous events, knowledge, and relationships. Returning to the death scene, seeking revenge, or using information from a past life is strictly forbidden. NLR ensures fairness and realism in roleplay. After dying you must wait 4 minutes before returning the scene.

Rule 5 | Fear Roleplay

Fear Roleplay ensures that your character reacts realistically to threats or dangerous situations. When someone points a weapon at you, you cannot charge, pull a weapon, or act recklessly. Failure to respond appropriately to threats may result in warnings or moderation action.

Rule 6 | Staff Final Say

Staff Members have the final say if it's during a mod scene or them giving you an order. Failure to comply will result in disciplinary action. Please note that staff may also moderate you for some stuff that isn't in the rules. If you feel like a moderation action is wrong, you may open a report.

Rule 7 | Safezone

Safezones are areas in the game where you should not be committing crimes in such as shooting, fleeing to, or doing a mass roleplay in. Areas in game that are considered a safezone are Civilian Spawns, Sheriff's Office, Police Station, Fire Departments, and lastly the DOT Station.

Rule 8 | Banned Roleplays

The following roleplays are banned from this server and if you are caught doing them you'll be banned. Suicide RP, self harm, bomb RP, trafficking, drugs, alcohol, tragic events, sexual/nudity, racism, protests/riots, and hate speech. Moderators do hold the power to kick and ban you if found doing anything like this. EMS is exempt from drug roleplay due to having medical drugs and protests may be allowed if approved by ownership.

Rule 9 | Priority Timers & Metagaming / Powergaming

Whenever the priority/peacetimer is on in game, you shall not commit any crimes, flee from law enforcement, shoot/kill players or damage vehicles.

Players may not use information gained from out of character in a roleplay. This includes zooming out with your mouse to see where LEO is, using freecam to see peoples location, and etc.

Rule 10 | Stolen Radio/Scanner & Other Agencies

Stolen Radio/Scanner isn't allowed even if you stole a radio from Law Enforcement officer.

Roleplaying as Agencies that are not within Florida State Roleplay Discord will lead to punishments. This means you cannot roleplay as ICE, Homeland Security, USMS, etc.

Rule 11 | Interfering with Scene

Interfering with a roleplay scene, moderation scene, or active roleplay without a legitimate purpose is prohibited. If you are not directly involved in the scene, do not enter the area or disrupt the roleplay.

Rule 12 | Pointless Pursuits & Water Evasion

Doing pursuits without a valid reason will result in moderation action. As well as fleeing into the water and staying under/in the way for more than 3 minutes is considered Water Evasion.

Rule 13 | Priority Request

If you ever wish to rob the bank, jewelry store, or kidnap someone/do a hostage scene you must get priority which you can do in the commands channel by running /priority request

Rule 14 | GTA Driving & Not Roleplaying Crashes

If you are just driving around not roleplaying any crashes or going over the speed of 120 MPH you will be moderated. Unless you are in a pursuit you may not go over the speeds of 120 MPH. As well you must be driving realistically at all times.

Rule 15 | Bunny Hopping & Gang Roleplay

If you are in a gun fight or being tased, you may not jump to avoid being shot / tase.

Groups of 4 or more are considered Gang Roleplay and require Directive Team approval. Gang RP must remain realistic and may not be used to disrupt or overwhelm other roleplays within Florida State Roleplay.

Tier 4 Platinum+ can have 7 people in their group but more than that total will result in it being Gang Roleplay
